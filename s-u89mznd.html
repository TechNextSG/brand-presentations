09 · do's and don'ts

The kit on one page.

Do

  • Navy fields, cream grounds, bronze and gold lines.
  • One orange CTA in view, navy-ink label.
  • Plus Jakarta Sans + Inter; italic bronze accent line.
  • The logo on white or a white panel; keep “Singapore” attached.
  • TRE™ in every visible line.
  • Offer both paths: provider or event, and the intake call.

Don't

  • The SVG approximation as the logo.
  • The full-colour logo straight on navy or a photo.
  • Eyebrows, gradient text, side stripes, bounce motion.
  • Clinical outcome promises.
  • Call it a licensee or chapter.
  • Use the Reiki certificate photo.

The summary to keep beside you.

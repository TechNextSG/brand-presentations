09 · do's and don'ts

The kit on one page.

Do

  • Warm light surfaces; marigold is the action colour.
  • Ink text on marigold; #8F5A00 for orange text.
  • Figtree everywhere, sentence case.
  • The two-bar accent once per layout.
  • The mark on a white tile over dark or photos.
  • TRE™ in every visible line.

Don't

  • Marigold or sun as text on light.
  • The full-colour mark straight on navy.
  • Recolour or shadow the bars.
  • Uppercase buttons.
  • Stock boardrooms.
  • The superseded navy/amber + Sora palette.

The summary to keep beside you.

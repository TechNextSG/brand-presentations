09Do's and don'ts

Do

  • Navy and gold on cream; navy only for bands, footers and event heroes.
  • One italic gold word per headline, in Playfair.
  • The all-gold wordmark with one wing-tip of clear space.
  • TRE™ in every visible line.
  • Real photos in natural light.
  • Outline buttons that fill from the pointer.

Don't

  • Two-tone the wordmark or separate the bird.
  • White text on gold.
  • TRE® or bare TRE in text.
  • Medical claims.
  • Emoji in headings.
  • Extra colours on posters (sage, terracotta…).

Diagnose before we prescribe

We ask how the business runs, then recommend: what fits, what can wait, and what not to buy.

Own the whole solution

One accountable team from the advice to go-live and support. No hand-offs.

Expertise you can name

Advisers by domain, a reason behind every recommendation, standard Odoo first.

  3. Fourteen solutions. We advise where to start.
  4. Five steps, the same advisers from start to finish.

  6. From lead to reconciled cash, across five departments.
  7. Four practices. One team.
  8. Every Odoo app, one database.
  9. Tell us what slows you down.
  10. Configure. Migrate. Go live. Keep going.
  11. Where Odoo runs is a scoping decision, not a default.

11Do's and don'ts

Do

  • Lead with #3167CA on white; light only.
  • Blue logo on light, white on dark, 25% clear space.
  • Jakarta 800 headlines with one blue marked phrase; Inter body; one Caveat kicker at most.
  • Label every demo screen as sample data.
  • Official Odoo icons in their own colours.
  • Social: 3:4, logo top-left, Odoo wordmark top-right.

Don't

  • Recolour, stretch, rotate or watermark the logo; use the old caps mark.
  • Use the wrong blues or Montserrat.
  • Put the partner badge on social posts.
  • Put Odoo icons on tiles, or tiles behind flat icons.
  • Redraw Nexi or cover her face.
  • Sci-fi hologram dashboards, peach, mint or purple grounds.

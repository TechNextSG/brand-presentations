03

Rules for all three

TRE™, always

The letters TRE carry ™ in all visible text. Never ®, never bare. File names, URLs and form values are exempt.

Light only

All three brands use light backgrounds. No dark-mode versions of pages or posts; navy appears as bands and footers.

Check before quoting

Dates and prices change. Check the live events page first, and keep the 2027 certification out of public material until Isabelle publishes it.

04

Co-branding

When two brands share one piece, one leads and the other supports.

Lead brand

Top left, larger, and its palette and font run the whole design. Pick it by audience: organisations = BHD Asia, individuals = HummingBeing, practitioners = TRE™ in Singapore.

Partner brand

Top right, smaller, in a white pill with "in partnership with". Use its horizontal lock-up and never recolour it to match.

Collaborations

Multi-organisation events (for example the Romania events with TRE România and ICF) keep every partner logo the client supplied. Don't strip one out.
